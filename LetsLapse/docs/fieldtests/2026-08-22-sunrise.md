# Field test — 2026-08-22 sunrise, three devices

**Arms:** iPad Air M1 (Auto/Latitude, EVERY Auto @3s — 991 frames, 49.5 min,
user-ended) · iPhone 16 Pro (Auto/Lumen — self-stopped at 372 frames /
18.7 min) · iPhone 12 Pro (five Auto runs, all self-stopped at 2/38/28/2/10
frames; then fixed-5 @5s — 399 frames / 34 min, survived with drops).
Sunrise: light RISING, every device pinned at its ISO floor all run.

Audit: `blend_compare.py` + `shoot_audit.py` (same protocol as
[2026-08-20-sunset.md](2026-08-20-sunset.md)).

## The actuation fix: CONFIRMED WORKING

Delivered exposure tracked the engine on every ramped run — iPhones within
0.05–0.14 stops (vs the sunset's silent 6-stop divergence). The residual
iPhone "divergence" is a cosmetic ISO-bookkeeping offset (commanded 33 →
EXIF 32; 54 → 50; 18 → 16): the device quantizes to a neighboring ISO and
EXIF reports that. The guard should tolerate ≤⅙ stop or compare against the
device-quantized value.

## Why runs self-stopped (adversarially verified; first hypothesis refuted)

**One kill switch:** `consecutiveProcessingFailures >= 3`
(LiveBlendRawController) — three consecutive ZERO-capture windows end the
run cleanly. Failed windows write no capture_log entries, so stopped runs
look failure-free in the travelling log. Two different diseases fed it:

1. **12 Pro (all five Auto deaths): processing-throughput starvation.**
   Blend+author medians ran 3.35–9.05s against a 3s interval (>100%
   utilization); the `pendingProcessingWindows >= 2` backpressure gate then
   stops firing captures, windows close EMPTY in phase-slip clusters, and
   any 3-streak kills the run. A FIFO simulation reproduces 46–48 of the 48
   observed failed windows. The thermal-probe-collision hypothesis was
   REFUTED for these runs (4 of 5 had zero mid-run thermal transitions).
   Root defect: Auto's probe measures CAPTURE fps but never blend
   throughput, so the ceiling books >100% processing on slow devices — and
   the guard counts the scheduler's own backpressure abstinence as
   "failures."
2. **16 Pro: capture refusal at the fair→serious thermal transition.** Its
   final window's bracket returned 5 explicit errors (the only nonzero
   frameFailures in all 8 runs) → `bracketDisabled` tripped → the degraded
   singles path then gated forever on `captureReadiness == .ready` → two
   empty windows completed the guard. Whether iOS refused RAW under
   pressure or the Auto-only mid-run re-profile probe collided is
   undecidable from sidecars (differential test: drive a fixed-depth 16 Pro
   run through a serious transition).

**Survivors:** iPad ran at 48% utilization (gate never engaged; 991/991
full windows). t6 fixed-5 had the same backlog disease at 102% utilization
but never exceeded a 2-streak — sub-threshold survival, not immunity.

## Reliability fixes required (auto-stop is not acceptable)

1. Backpressure-starved empty windows must NOT count toward the stop guard
   — count only genuine capture errors. A slow device should degrade
   (shallower blends, longer effective cadence), never abandon a shoot.
2. The Auto ceiling must respect PROCESSING throughput: measure blend time
   (probe extension or live: shrink ceiling while processing median >
   interval).
3. Fix the post-`bracketDisabled` readiness deadlock (singles must fire on
   the non-responsive pipeline).
4. Persist the stop reason: `Session.endReason` + failed-window counters in
   capture_log.json, surfaced in the app. (LLog is print-only in the
   field.)
5. iPad duration-latch floor: custom-exposure durations below ~1/253s never
   latched (delivered took only 5 discrete values all run), leaving the
   final 35 min up to 1.13 stops brighter than commanded → highlight clip
   climbed to ~30% (80% of sky ≥250 at 05:10). The new divergence guard
   caught it (389/991 windows >0.5 honestly logged). Needs an on-device
   repro + a re-apply/quantize-latch strategy.
6. Guard cosmetics: subtract the ISO-quantization offset so healthy runs
   log near-zero divergence.

## Strategy verdict on the user's metrics

Sunrise collapsed the strategies to pure blend count (ISO pinned at floor,
exposure owned by the shared engine): Zone 5, Latitude 7, Lumen 5+boost 0
(no starving shadows in rising light) — on every device, per the shadow
logs.

- **Motion blur (bigger = better):** Latitude "wins" (0.98% of interval
  integrated vs 0.29%/0.09%) — but ALL runs sit under 1% vs a ~50%
  film-look target, and brackets are sensor-consecutive (~34ms), so motion
  renders as a micro-trail. **No count strategy can move this metric
  materially.** The lever is integration coverage: long shutters (ND) or
  continuous stacking (the video/JPEG path integrates ~100% of the window
  at 30fps — structurally superior for this metric).
- **Noise (less = better):** no strategy separation. Measured stacking gain
  inside t6 was only 1.3–1.6× (correlated noise floor), so Latitude's
  7-vs-5 margin is <0.2 stops in practice.
- **Exposure balance:** a run/device attribute, not a strategy attribute.
  16 Pro best while alive (clip ≤0.95%); t6 most exposure-honest but
  rendered ~2 stops dark (sky-protecting AE target → 50–60% shadow clip);
  iPad's late clipping is the latch floor, not Latitude.
- **Visuals:** 16 Pro (Lumen arm) produced the best frames of the
  transition but died before the payoff. The iPad's "looked best live"
  impression is REFUTED in the DNGs (blown skies from ~04:49). 12 Pro owns
  the best late skies. t6's drops confirmed: 7 short windows / 19 frames
  lost, all in the serious-thermal tail, three windows split into
  visible duplicate-frame pairs with a one-frame noise pulse.

## Disposition

- The strategy layer is not where quality is being won or lost at sunrise;
  its differentiation regime is falling/dark light (see the sunset test —
  Lumen's pixel signal). Keep the layer cheap: Zone default, Lumen as
  boost + ramp watchdog. Latitude has not yet earned its complexity in
  either test; give it one clean sunset before deciding.
- The real pivots: (1) reliability fixes above — capture infrastructure,
  before any strategy work matters; (2) for the motion-blur goal, a new
  axis — integration coverage via the video-path stacking (JPEG) or ND —
  not another blend-count curve.
