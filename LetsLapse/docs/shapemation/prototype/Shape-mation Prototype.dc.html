Assumptions and context

Built from the prototype brief (2026-09-20), the macOS mirrors docs/design/macOS/shapemation.builder.*.svg and the scene kit's manifest.json. The seven shipped steps are recreated at the mirrors' geometry (560 pt sheet, 47.5 pt nav bar, 16 pt padding, llCard radius 18, the pinned 78.5 pt bottom bar). The Sequence board is a new step between Mode and Timing; it is the one place the sheet grows — to 900 × 720 pt — because the board wants ~560 pt of thumbnails beside a 280 pt column for the charts and the selected photo. At 560 the charts had to go under the board and the reject-and-watch loop lost its glance.

Every badge, number and rendered thumbnail on the list, the board, the scrub and the player comes from one file, model.js — §4 written once: cover fit, the natural position, the running-median path, the per-side zoom z, crop risk f = 1 − 1/z², Σ J and mean crop. Fixed face runs the shipped output-frame math with the ×2 rasterisation rule. Nothing is detected: the manifest's bbox is the truth.

The kit's compositions stand in for a library. Their tags are the manifest's own fields (sequence prefix, scene, sky, tram angle), plus one synthetic tag, Bad apple, on the ten photos §3 names — the way Steven's "Shape testing" tag works. The Portrait 30 set is the aspect filter, not a tag. Capture order is the manifest's order. Titles are the composition ids; the app would show "Photo 13. 9. at 12:57".

Timing with a per-photo hold, Collections with re-render, the three board forms (grid · strip · list), exposed thresholds, the phone pass and the shape-inventory stretch are all in. The Decisions tab computes the board's two numbers live for the four sets, with and without auto-reject, so the log is never stale against the prototype.

Hand-off — what to build, in what order
Four buckets. Shipped is already in the Swift app and is recreated here unchanged; touch only what the row says. Required is the new work the brief's §1 goal cannot be met without. Open is what the prototype could not settle on synthetic photos and hands to the developer to investigate on real libraries and recommend. Enhancement improves the flow but the goal survives without it; build after Required, or cut. The prototype's code is not carried over — the developer works from this table, the States and Decisions tabs, model.js as the reference for the maths, and the SVG mirrors it redraws.
